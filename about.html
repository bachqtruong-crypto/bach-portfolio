Who I Am

I’m an average tech enjoyer who solves problems and helps people make sense of technology. Over the past few years, I’ve worked in large, fast-paced environments, from supporting a university campus to systems used across global healthcare networks.

At UTSA, I supported students, faculty, and staff by troubleshooting everything from Wi-Fi issues to account access and security concerns. I also ran wireless site surveys and helped identify performance issues across different locations to improve campus connectivity.

In my role supporting the Department of Defense and Defense Health Agency, I worked in a 24/7 environment where reliability really mattered. I handled a wide range of technical issues, from network outages to system access and device support, while using tools like Active Directory and AWS services. Security was a big part of the role, including encryption, certificates, and secure authentication.

Earlier in my career, I built a strong foundation in IT support by working hands-on with networking issues, devices, and end users. That’s where I really developed my troubleshooting mindset and learned how to communicate technical problems in ways that make sense.

Lately, I’ve been focusing on AWS and cloud technologies, building my own projects and documenting them on this site. My goal is to transition into a cloud-focused role where I can combine my support background with cloud engineering and security. I’m always learning, building, and looking for ways to improve, so this site is a place where I can share that journey.

Background & Education

I have a Bachelor of Business Administration in Cyber Security from UTSA and hold CompTIA Security+. I focus on cloud security, infrastructure, and automation.
